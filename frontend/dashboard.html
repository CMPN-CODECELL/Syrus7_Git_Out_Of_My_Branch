Website User Guide

Welcome to Shani Option Chain Analytics. This dashboard replicates the official National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) equity derivatives option chain layout, enhanced with real-time derivative valuations, theoretical Black-Scholes-Merton (BSM) prices, and real-time Greek computations.

Dynamic Search & Filters

Use the search box in the top filter bar to look up active stock symbols or indices (e.g., NIFTY, RELIANCE, TCS) traded on the NSE. The system auto-completes and fetches active parameters. Filter option expiry dates or isolate specific strikes from the drop-downs.

Black-Scholes-Merton Calculator

Adjust variables in the BSM Control Panel to simulate pricing environments:

  • Spot Price: Base underlying asset value (supports slide scaling).
  • Volatility (σ %): Standard deviation of underlying returns.
  • Days to Expiry: Time remaining until derivative settlement.
  • Risk-Free Rate: Prevailing interest rate environment (e.g. 6.50%).
  • Dividend Yield: Continuous cash payouts of stock or index.

Option Chain Shading & Pricing Highlights

ITM Shading: Pale yellow highlights show in-the-money strikes (calls under spot, puts above spot).

Underpriced / Overpriced Fields (BS PRICE): Comparing BSM fair price with market Last Traded Price (LTP):

  • Teal Green: Market price is Underpriced compared to BSM model (Theoretical Edge available).
  • Orange Red: Market price is Overpriced compared to BSM model.

Strike Greeks Analysis

Click on any Strike Price cell or LTP value to launch the Greeks Inspection Modal. This reveals detailed mathematical parameters (d₁, d₂, N(d₁), N(d₂)) and active model outputs (Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta, Rho) for both Call and Put contracts on that specific strike.

Technical Indicators & Market Signals

The Technicals tab computes quantitative signals, trend indicators, and floor pivot levels:

  • RSI (14): Relative Strength Index. Values ≥70 signal Overbought (Bearish alert), ≤30 signal Oversold (Bullish alert).
  • MACD (12, 26, 9): Moving Average Convergence Divergence. Crossovers above signal line indicate Bullish trend.
  • Bollinger Bands (20, 2): 20-period moving average with ±2 standard deviation envelopes.
  • Moving Averages (SMA & EMA 20/50): Simple and Exponential moving averages. Spot ≥ MA indicates Bullish bias.
  • ATR (14): Average True Range measuring price volatility amplitude.
  • Floor Pivots: Intraday Pivot (P), Resistance (R1-R3), and Support (S1-S3) levels calculated from prior price history.
  • Composite Score: Aggregates 7 indicator signals into overall bias (Strong Bullish, Bullish, Neutral, Bearish, Strong Bearish).
